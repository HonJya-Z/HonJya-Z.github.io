Basic Info

Hey there! I was born in Harbin, a gorgeous city in China's northeast—Heilongjiang Province! ❄️ (・ω・)

Before starting school, I also spent my times in Kanagawa, Japan.

I grew up mainly in Nanjing, Jiangsu until I turned 18. As a former capital of six dynasties and a cultural hub, Nanjing is one of the city that truly represents China—and it's the place I feel most connected to even now!

After becoming an adult, I moved to Dalian, Liaoning for university and also had the chance to live and study in Kyoto, Japan for a while.

I'm a native bilingual—since childhood, I've been fluent in both Chinese and Japanese. It still amazes me how I can speak Japanese without even thinking! It's totally different from learning English later on.

Now, I can handle four languages: Chinese, Japanese, English, and Russian!

Though, I have to admit—my spoken English and Russian could use some practice. So, if you'd like to chat and help me improve, I'd be super grateful! (´▽｀)

🔬 Research Interests

My passion lies at the exciting intersection of fundamental physics and data-driven discovery. I'm particularly fascinated by cosmic mysteries like fast radio bursts and dark matter, which can be probed through electromagnetic and cosmic-ray observations 🌌.

I find the process of unraveling these puzzles through data analysis and algorithm design incredibly rewarding. This path naturally aligns with my enjoyment of scientific programming and problem-solving in Unix-like environments (I'm a dedicated macOS and Linux user!).

After exploring various physics branches during my undergraduate studies, I've solidified my desire to pursue a future in astronomical observation and data analysis.

🛠️Skills

Python, C, MATLAB, LaTeX, VASP, LAMMPS, Materials Studio, COMSOL, Linux, HTML, Origin, etc.
